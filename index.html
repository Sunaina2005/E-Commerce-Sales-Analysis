Key Insights
🏆
APAC is the #1 Market
Asia-Pacific leads with $3.59M (28.4%) of total revenue, significantly ahead of EU at $2.94M. APAC also delivers the highest absolute profit at $436K.
⚡
Technology Dominates Sales
Technology products generate the highest revenue at $4.74M with the best profit margin of 14.0%, compared to Furniture's 6.9% — highlighting a margin disparity worth addressing.
📉
EMEA: Revenue-Profit Gap
EMEA generates $806K in sales but only $44K profit (5.4% margin) — the lowest margin of all markets. High shipping costs or discounting may be eroding profitability in this region.
🚀
Strong 4-Year Growth
Revenue grew from $2.26M in 2011 to $4.30M in 2014 — a 90% increase over 4 years, demonstrating consistent market expansion and scaling operations.
🎯
Consumer Segment Rules
Consumers account for 51.5% of all revenue at $6.51M. However, Corporate customers show a slightly higher profit margin, suggesting potential for B2B strategy growth.
📬
Standard Class Dominates
59.9% of orders ship via Standard Class ($7.58M). Same Day delivery represents only 5.3% of revenue, indicating an opportunity to upsell premium shipping options.
